Frame-by-frame transcript

Frames 0 to 149. Rings and links. The stationary swash plate does not rotate. An anti-drive link holds it. The rotating swash plate is driven with the mast. A section plane opens through the uniball sleeve.

Frames 150 to 299. Collective translation. The whole swash plate rises. In this illustrated linkage every blade feather increases together.

Frames 300 to 509. Cyclic tilt. The stationary ring tilts and holds that tilt in the helicopter frame while the rotating ring turns. Each pitch link rises and falls once per revolution. Tilt is exaggerated.

Frames 510 to 749. One pitch link is followed into the pitch horn and the blade feathering axis. Scale is a closer viewpoint, not a change in part size. Rotor rate is slowed.

Frames 750 to 1019. The camera returns to the complete rotor and holds. The violet plot is mechanical feather versus azimuth. Rotor-disc tilt is not drawn, because that phase is design-dependent.
